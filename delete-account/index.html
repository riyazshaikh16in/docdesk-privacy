Delete your account

DocDesk · com.rslabs.docdesk

This page explains how a doctor can delete their DocDesk account and everything stored with it. It applies to both the Android app and the web app.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open DocDesk and sign in with your mobile number.
  2. Go to More → Settings and scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Tip: export your data first from Reports → Export all data if you want a copy.

Option 2 — by email

Email riyazshaikh16in@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my DocDesk account” and the mobile number you sign in with, in international format (for example +919876543210). We may reply once to confirm the request came from you; nothing is deleted until we are sure.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Clinic profile, fee and receipt settingsDeleted

DocDesk subscriptionAuto-renew cancelled; billing history deleted
Internal support notes and access logs about your clinicDeleted
Your login (mobile number)Deleted from Firebase Authentication
One-way hash of your mobile number + free-trial end dateKept, only to prevent a repeat free trial. It cannot be turned back into your number.

Payment records that Razorpay is legally required to keep for DocDesk subscription payments are retained by Razorpay under its own policy.

How long it takes

In-app deletion happens immediately. Email requests are actioned within 30 days, usually much sooner, and we reply to confirm when done.

Deletion is permanent. A deleted account cannot be restored. Signing up again with the same number creates a new, empty clinic without a new free trial.

Patients of a clinic

If you are a patient and want your details removed, ask your doctor. If you cannot reach them, email us with the clinic's name and your mobile number and we will pass the request on.
