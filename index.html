Privacy Policy

DocDesk by RS Labs · Last updated 27 September 2026

DocDesk helps a doctor run a clinic's front desk: give patients daily tokens, run the OPD queue, show the queue on a waiting-room screen, collect consultation fees and share receipts. This page says exactly what we store, why, who else handles it, and how to get it deleted. It covers the Android app (com.rslabs.docdesk) and the web app at docdesk-rslabs.web.app.

Who is responsible for what

DocDesk has one kind of user: the doctor (clinic owner). The doctor decides which patient details to enter and is responsible for having their patients' permission to do so. RS Labs stores and processes that information only on the doctor's behalf, to provide the app.

What we collect about the doctor

  • Mobile number — used only to sign you in with a one-time code (OTP). The SMS is sent by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Clinic profile — clinic name, doctor's name and qualification, clinic address and phone, consultation fee and receipt settings. These appear on receipts.
  • Subscription status — your free-trial dates and, if you subscribe, the Razorpay reference for each DocDesk payment.

What the doctor stores about patients

  • Patient details — name, and optionally mobile number, age, gender and address.
  • Visit and fee records — visit dates, token numbers, whether the patient was seen, fees, payments received (amount, cash / UPI / card, an optional reference) and receipt numbers.

DocDesk does not store medical records. There are no fields for symptoms, diagnoses, prescriptions, test results, vitals, attachments or clinical notes. DocDesk never stores full card numbers, UPI PINs or bank details.

The waiting-room display

If the doctor turns on the TV queue display, DocDesk creates a private, hard-to-guess link that shows the clinic name, the doctor's name, the current and upcoming token numbers and patient names, and the list of missed tokens. Phone numbers and fees are never shown there. Anyone who has the link can see that screen, so the doctor controls who gets it (for example, a TV in the waiting room, or patients who receive it with their token). The doctor can reset the link at any time, which stops the old link from working immediately.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, and no selling or renting of data.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not message patients. Token numbers and receipts are sent by the doctor from their own WhatsApp or share sheet; DocDesk only prepares the message or PDF.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Mumbai, India, encrypted in transit and at rest by Google.
  • Each clinic's records can be read only by that doctor's signed-in account; this is enforced by server-side security rules. The only exception is the waiting-room display described above.
  • RS Labs can see account, clinic and patient records through an internal, access-controlled admin console. We use it only to run and support the service — for example account and trial status, usage statistics, fixing problems you report, preventing abuse and handling deletion requests. Every access is logged, and we never use patient details to contact patients or for marketing.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting) — to run the app. Firebase privacy.
  • Razorpay — only if the doctor buys a DocDesk subscription. Razorpay collects the payment details on its own page; we receive only the payment status and reference. Razorpay privacy.

How long we keep it

  • Everything is kept while the account exists, so the clinic can see its full history.
  • When the doctor deletes the account, all clinic data, patient records and the display link are deleted immediately.
  • We keep a one-way hash of the doctor's phone number and the free-trial end date after deletion, only to stop the free trial being claimed again. It cannot be turned back into the phone number.
  • Payment records that Razorpay must keep by law are retained by Razorpay under its own policy.

Deleting data

  • Doctors: in the app, open More → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes the clinic, all patients, visits and receipts, the display link, cancels any DocDesk subscription, and removes the login. See how to delete your account for the other options.
  • Patients: ask your doctor to remove your details. If you cannot reach them, email us (below) with the clinic's name and your mobile number and we will pass the request to the doctor.
  • Doctors can export all their data at any time from Reports → Export all data.

Children

DocDesk is a tool for doctors and is not directed at children. A clinic may register patients under 18; the doctor is responsible for having a parent or guardian's consent.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will update the date at the top of this page and mention it in the app's release notes.
